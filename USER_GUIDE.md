# Practice Recorder v16 — Step-by-Step Instructions

The Practice Recorder helps you sing along with one learning track, record your voice, and compare your recording with a second track.

Your music and recording stay on your device. The recorder does not upload them.

## Make the words larger

At the top of the recorder, choose one of these buttons:

- **Normal:** standard-size words
- **Large:** words that are 25% larger
- **Extra Large:** words that are 50% larger

The recorder remembers your choice on that device. You can change it at any time.

Your browser or device may also have its own text-size, page-zoom, and accessibility settings. Those settings can be used together with the recorder's text-size buttons.

## Before you begin

You need:

- A phone, tablet, or computer connected to the internet
- Earbuds or headphones
- The two practice-track files saved on your device

The two tracks are usually:

1. **Track to sing against:** the recording with your part missing
2. **Track to compare with:** the recording with your part sung alone or louder than the other parts

## If you are using an iPad

Chrome is recommended. Safari may prevent you from changing the track volume while the microphone is active.

Before opening the Practice Recorder:

1. Connect your wired or Bluetooth earbuds or headset.
2. Play some music and make sure you hear it through the earbuds or headset.
3. Stop the music.
4. Open Chrome.
5. Open the Practice Recorder.

Keep the headset connected until you finish practicing. If you change or reconnect the headset, close and reopen the recorder before recording again.

## Allow microphone access

The first time you press **Record**, the browser should ask for permission to use the microphone. Choose **Allow**.

This permission is only for recording your voice in the Practice Recorder. Your recording is not uploaded.

### If Chrome says microphone access is blocked on an iPad

1. Open the iPad **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Privacy & Security**.
3. Tap **Microphone**.
4. Make sure **Chrome** is turned on.
5. Return to Chrome.
6. Reload the Practice Recorder page.
7. Press **Record** and choose **Allow** if asked.

If you see a microphone symbol beside Chrome’s address bar, tap it and make sure permission is turned on for the recorder’s website.

### If Safari says microphone access is blocked on an iPad

1. Open the Practice Recorder in Safari.
2. Tap the Page Menu button on the left side of the address bar.
3. Open **Website Settings**.
4. Set **Microphone** to **Allow**.
5. Reload the page.
6. Press **Record** again.

## White River Sound members: finding downloaded chorus files

This section is only for White River Sound chorus members who obtain practice tracks from HarmonySite or [whiteriversoundchorus.org](https://whiteriversoundchorus.org). If you received your tracks from another choir, teacher, or source, skip to **Choose the two tracks** below.

On an iPhone, the folder depends on how you downloaded the files:

- From the **HarmonySite app:** **Files → On My iPhone → HarmonySite**
- From the White River Sound website in **Chrome:** **Files → On My iPhone → Chrome**
- From the White River Sound website in **Safari:** **Files → On My iPhone → Downloads**

Use the same download method each time if possible. This makes your practice tracks easier to find because they should continue going to the same folder.

These locations have been confirmed on an iPhone. They may be different on an iPad, and Android file locations have not yet been confirmed. The Practice Recorder also works for people who are not White River Sound members; they should select their audio files from wherever those files were saved on their device.

## Choose the two tracks

### 1. Choose the track to sing against

1. Find **Track to sing against**.
2. Tap **Choose file**.
3. Find the correct audio file on your device.
4. Tap the file.
5. Check that its name appears after **Loaded:**.

### 2. Choose the track to compare with

1. Find **Track to compare with**.
2. Tap **Choose file**.
3. Find the correct audio file on your device.
4. Tap the file.
5. Check that its name appears after **Loaded:**.

When both files have loaded, the recorder will say **Ready to record**.

The recorder saves local copies of these two tracks in this browser. The next time you use the same browser, device, and website address, the tracks should load automatically.

To select a different track, tap **Change file** under that track.

## Record your voice

1. Put on your earbuds or headphones.
2. Make sure the learning track is playing through them, not through the device speaker.
3. To begin at the start of the song, leave **Record from** at `0:00`.
4. To practice a later section, drag **Record from** to the desired starting time.
5. Tap **Record**.
6. Choose **Allow** if the browser asks to use the microphone.
7. Sing your part while the first track plays.
8. Tap **Stop** when you finish.
9. Wait while the recorder processes your recording.

Do not close the page while recording or processing.

## Check that your voice was recorded

After processing, look under **Compare**.

If you see **Microphone audio captured from…**, the recorder detected sound from the named microphone.

If you see a warning that the recording contains little or no microphone sound:

1. Check that the headset is fully connected.
2. Make sure its microphone is not muted.
3. Close other apps that might be using the microphone.
4. Close and reopen the recorder.
5. Record a short test while speaking loudly.

The microphone named by the recorder may be the iPad microphone even when a headset is connected. The browser and iPadOS choose which microphone the website receives.

## Compare your voice with the reference track

1. Tap **Compare**.
2. Listen to your voice and the reference track together.
3. Use **My voice** to make your recording louder or quieter.
4. Use **Reference** to make the comparison track louder or quieter.

To hear only your recorded voice:

1. Move **Reference** to `0%`.
2. Leave **My voice** at `100%` or higher.
3. Tap **Compare** or **Replay**.

To hear only the reference track, move **My voice** to `0%`.

## Comparison controls

- **Compare:** Start listening from the position shown by the Playback slider.
- **Replay:** Return to the point where the recording began and play again.
- **Pause:** Pause the comparison.
- **Resume:** Continue after pausing.
- **Stop:** Stop the comparison and return to its starting point.
- **Playback:** Choose where you want comparison playback to begin.
- **Record Again:** Discard the current recording and make another one.

Your microphone recording lasts only until you close or reload the page. The two selected practice tracks can be remembered, but your recorded voice is not saved for the next session.

## Align your voice with the reference

Your device may add a small delay while recording. Use **Voice timing** if your voice and the reference do not sound synchronized.

- If your voice sounds late, tap an **Earlier** button.
- If your voice sounds early, tap a **Later** button.
- Use the `20 ms` buttons for small changes.
- Use the `100 ms` buttons for larger changes.
- Tap **Reset** to return to `0 ms`.

The timing changes immediately while comparison is playing.

Leave **Remember this timing correction on this device** checked if you want the recorder to use the same correction next time.

## Remove the remembered tracks

Tap **Forget saved files** to remove the recorder’s saved copies.

This does not delete the original files from your device. Tracks already loaded remain available until you close or reload the page.

## Troubleshooting

### The recorder says microphone access was blocked

Allow microphone access for the browser and the recorder’s website. Follow the Chrome or Safari permission steps near the beginning of this guide.

### The recorder says no microphone was found

1. Reconnect the headset or microphone.
2. Confirm that another recording app can hear it.
3. Close that other app.
4. Reload the Practice Recorder.
5. Try again.

### The browser could not use the microphone

1. Close other apps and browser tabs that may be using audio.
2. Disconnect and reconnect the headset.
3. Completely close the browser.
4. Open the browser and recorder again.

### The track plays through the iPad speaker

1. Stop recording immediately to prevent the microphone from recording the track.
2. Close the recorder.
3. Connect the headset before reopening the browser.
4. Confirm that other music plays through the headset.
5. Reopen the recorder and try a short test.

### Safari volume will not change while recording

This is a Safari/iPadOS limitation. Use Chrome on that iPad.

### I hear the reference but not my voice

1. Set **Reference** to `0%`.
2. Set **My voice** to `100%` or higher.
3. Tap **Replay**.
4. Read the microphone-capture message under **Compare**.
5. If it reports little or no microphone sound, reconnect the headset and make a new test recording.

### The remembered tracks disappeared

Tracks can disappear if you use private browsing, clear browser data, change browsers, use a different device, or open the recorder at a different website address. Choose the original files again.

## A good first test

Before practicing a full song:

1. Choose two short practice tracks.
2. Set **Record from** to `0:00`.
3. Record yourself speaking or singing loudly for about ten seconds.
4. Stop the recording.
5. Check the microphone-capture message.
6. Set **Reference** to `0%`.
7. Tap **Compare**.
8. Confirm that you hear your voice.
9. Restore **Reference** to `100%` and compare both tracks together.

Once this test works, begin your normal practice session.

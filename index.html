How it works

Before recording, use Record from to choose where in the first track you want to begin. During recording, the first track plays from that point while your microphone records your voice. During comparison, your recording is aligned to the same musical position in the second track. The Voice Timing controls shift your recording relative to the reference, changes are heard immediately during comparison, and the chosen timing can be remembered automatically on this device.
